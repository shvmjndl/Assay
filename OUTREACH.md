# Outreach

**Rule:** never send a template that could go to anyone. Cite one real technical decision every time.

---

## Example — Carson Gross / htmx (Issue #1)

Subject: Assay + your hypermedia bet

> Carson — I’ve been reading the HDA essays and your note on the HN thread about the decade before the 2023 spike. The part that stuck: rewriting intercooler without jQuery in 2020 not as a growth hack, but because you’d realized the project was about completing HTML as hypertext.
>
> I’m starting Assay — a weekly profile of long-running work with a paper trail (architecture, stuck periods, the one hard tradeoff). Opposite of launch-day directories. htmx is the clearest Issue #1 I can point at: ten years of architectural sympathy while SPA fashion ran the other direction.
>
> Would you be open to a longer profile — mostly drawn from what’s already public, plus a short pass with you on “what you’d do differently,” optional metrics, and an open roadmap? Happy to send a draft first so you can kill anything that feels off.
>
> — [name]

---

## Pattern that works

1. **Specific artifact** — essay, commit, changelog entry, HN comment  
2. **Why it fits Assay** — one sentence, craft not flattery  
3. **Ask** — permission to expand into the template; offer draft control  
4. **Short** — under ~150 words  

Expected reply rate when specific: much higher than “love to feature you.”
